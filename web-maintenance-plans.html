LEGAL

Service agreement terms

These terms apply to any Web Maintenance & Support engagement and form part of the agreement between the client and Algorithm Agency.

1.1 · PAYMENT & BILLING

Retainer fees are billed monthly in advance and payable within 7 days of invoice date. All prices are quoted in ZAR and exclude VAT.

1.2 · TERM & CANCELLATION

Engagements run month-to-month with no lock-in period. Either party may cancel with 30 days' written notice; no cancellation fee applies.

1.3 · SCOPE OF HOURS

Included hours cover content and image uploads, small fixes, and bug fixes only. Hours do not roll over between months and are billed at the standard hourly rate once exceeded.

1.4 · HOSTING & BACKUPS

Where the client hosts the site, backup frequency depends on that host's own policy. Where Algorithm Agency hosts, backups run daily as standard, and hosting is priced per site once its type and requirements are confirmed.

1.5 · RESPONSE TIMES

Response times apply during business hours (Mon–Fri, 08:00–17:00 SAST). Emergency or down-site response is treated as same business day regardless of tier.

1.6 · TIER CHANGES

Clients may move up or down a tier at any monthly renewal date. Changes take effect from the start of the next billing cycle.

1.7 · RATE REVIEWS

Retainer fees and the standard hourly rate are reviewed annually. Any change is communicated at least 30 days in advance of the next billing cycle.

1.8 · LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

Algorithm Agency's liability under this agreement is limited to the fees paid in the preceding three months, and excludes indirect or consequential loss.
